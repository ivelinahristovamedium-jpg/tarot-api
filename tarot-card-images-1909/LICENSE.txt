Card images

The 78 images are scans of the original Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck,
drawn by Pamela Colman Smith to A. E. Waite's instructions and published by
William Rider & Son, London, in 1909. The scans are Holly Voley's, as
uploaded to Wikimedia Commons in 2008; Taro's Tarot added the black frame.
They are not the 1971 U.S. Games Systems recolouring, which has its own
copyright.

The artwork is in the public domain: published in 1909 (public domain in
the United States), and Pamela Colman Smith died in 1951 (public domain in
the UK and EU since 1 January 2022). You may use the images for anything.
Credit is appreciated: "Card images via Taro's Tarot (https://tarostarot.com/tarot-card-data)".

Rider-Waite is a trademark of U.S. Games Systems, Inc. The name is used here
only to say which deck the scans come from.
